Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

CCTV Sync is a personal project that uploads CCTV recordings to a specific Google Drive folder selected or configured by the user. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information the app uses

The app processes the CCTV recordings you choose to upload and uses Google Drive authorization to place those recordings in the configured folder. The app is intended to run on your own device. The project owner does not operate a separate server that receives or stores your recordings or Google account data.

Google account authorization is used only to provide the Drive functionality you request. The exact access available to the app depends on the permissions shown on Google's consent screen. The app is intended to upload recordings to the specific folder configured by you; review the consent screen and app settings before granting access.

How information is used and shared

Recordings are uploaded to your Google Drive so you can store them there. The project owner does not sell, rent, or share your recordings or Google account data with third parties. Google processes information as necessary to provide Google Drive and OAuth services under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

The public project website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information when you visit the site according to GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Storage and retention

The app runs on your device; the project owner does not keep a separate copy of your recordings or account data. Recordings you upload are stored in your Google Drive and remain subject to your Google account and Drive settings. Local recordings and any app data on your device remain under your control.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Stop future uploads by stopping or powering off the device running CCTV Sync.
  • Delete uploaded recordings from the configured Google Drive folder using Google Drive.
  • Delete local recordings and any local app data from the device using that device's normal file-management tools.
  • Revoke the app's Google account access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Stopping the device prevents future app activity; it does not automatically delete files already uploaded to Google Drive or remove local files.

Security

Keep the device running the app secure and restrict access to its local files and Google account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the broklish GitHub profile.
